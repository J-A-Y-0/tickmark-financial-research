## Summary

Harbor Point holds a Neutral house rating on Lyft with a fictional 12-month price target of $15. Lyft has turned profitable, but it is a United States and Canada ridesharing business competing against a larger, more diversified rival.

## What the 10-K shows

Lyft reported fiscal 2024 revenue of $5,786.0 million, up 31%, and Gross Bookings of $16,099.4 million, up 17%. Rides grew 17% to 828.3 million. Net income was $22.8 million, Lyft's first full-year profit, and Adjusted EBITDA was $382.4 million, a margin of 2.4% of Gross Bookings.

## Model assumptions

- Rides growth of 12% in 2025, slowing to 9% in 2026.
- Adjusted EBITDA margin of 2.8% of Gross Bookings in 2025 and 3.1% in 2026.
- Insurance cost per ride flat in 2025 after two years of increases.

## Why Neutral

Lyft's margin is less than half of Uber's on a Gross Bookings basis, and it has no delivery business to share fixed costs. Upside depends on further insurance relief and on whether the autonomous vehicle partnerships produce real supply.
