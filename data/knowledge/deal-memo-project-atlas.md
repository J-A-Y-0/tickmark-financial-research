## Restricted

This memo is restricted to portfolio managers. It describes a fictional private transaction and must not appear in research notes.

## Opportunity

Project Atlas is a proposed $40 million minority investment in a fictional private fleet-electrification company that leases electric vehicles to rideshare drivers. The company reported fictional 2024 revenue of $61 million and is growing about 70% a year.

## Proposed terms

- Investment of $40 million for an 18% stake, a pre-money valuation of about $182 million.
- One board seat and standard minority protections.
- The investment committee vote is scheduled for the third quarter of 2025.

## Rationale and risks

Rideshare platforms are pushing drivers toward electric vehicles, and drivers need financing. The main risks are concentration, because two rideshare platforms supply most of the company's customers, and battery residual values.
