## Summary

Our sector view on US delivery is positive. Order growth has normalised after the pandemic, but the leaders now grow profitably, and grocery and retail delivery widen the market beyond restaurants.

## DoorDash

DoorDash grew Marketplace GOV 20% to $80.2 billion in 2024 and Total Orders 20% to 2.6 billion. Revenue rose 24% to $10.7 billion, and Adjusted EBITDA was $1.9 billion, 2.4% of Marketplace GOV. We treat DoorDash as the share leader in US restaurant delivery and expect its take rate to rise slowly as advertising grows.

## Uber Delivery

Uber Delivery benefits from cross-selling to Mobility users through the Uber One membership. We view Uber and DoorDash as a rational duopoly in the United States.

## What would change our view

- A regulatory cap on delivery fees in a large city or state.
- Price competition from a well-funded new entrant.
- Grocery delivery margins staying negative beyond 2026.
