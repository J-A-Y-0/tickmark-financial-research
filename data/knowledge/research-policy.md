## Purpose

This policy sets how Harbor Point research cites its sources. It applies to research notes, investment committee materials and any AI-assisted research.

## Rules

1. Every reported figure must cite the primary source, normally the company's Form 10-K or 10-Q, with the section it came from.
2. House estimates must be labelled as estimates and kept separate from reported figures.
3. Web sources may be used for news and context, but never as the only source for a reported financial figure.
4. Material non-public information must never appear in research notes. Deal memos are restricted to portfolio managers.
5. AI-assisted answers must show their sources, and an analyst must check any figure before it is used in a published note.
